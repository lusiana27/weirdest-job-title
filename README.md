Entry for the **Weirdest Job Title Blind Judge Showdown** on make.fun — one line, 45 characters, zero mercy for the remote control that always vanishes between the couch cushions.

## The title

> **CEO of the Department of Lost Remote Controls**

A job title that sounds like a corner office and a government department, until you read what the department actually does: nothing but lose remote controls. Bureaucracy meets the most universal household mystery there is.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the one-line entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

```bash
python3 -S main.py
```
